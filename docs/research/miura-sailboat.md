# Miura Sailboat - Contextual Information

## Overview
The Miura 31 is a successful South African racer-cruiser designed by Oswald Berkemeyer and built by Nebe Boatworks beginning in 1978. It was Berkemeyer's second design after the Flamenca 25 and has achieved notable success as a coastal cruiser and offshore vessel.

## Specifications

### Dimensions
- **LOA (Length Overall)**: 31.00 ft / 9.45 m
- **LWL (Length Waterline)**: 28.00 ft / 8.53 m
- **Beam**: 10.00 ft / 3.05 m
- **Draft**: 5.00 ft / 1.52 m (max)

### Weight & Ballast
- **Displacement**: 10,700 lbs / 4,853 kg
- **Ballast**: 4,000 lbs / 1,814 kg
- **Classification**: Moderate weight sailboat

### Rigging
- **Type**: Masthead Sloop
- **Hull Type**: Fin keel with transom-hung rudder
- **Mast**: Single-spreader, relatively low profile (~45 ft above water)
- **Sail Plan**: Large genoa with smaller mainsail (most power from headsail)

## Design Characteristics

### Distinctive Features
- **Pronounced tumblehome** at the beam, providing:
  - Enhanced structural strength
  - Favorable IOR (International Offshore Rule) rating
  - Increased cabin space

### Notable Achievement
The Royal Cape Town Yacht Club has noted that more circumnavigations have been completed with Miura sailboats than with any other class of sailboat - a testament to the design's seaworthiness and reliability.

## Autotiller/Autopilot Relevance

### Steering System
- **Type**: Tiller-steered vessel
- **Rudder**: Transom-hung configuration

### Typical Autopilot Specifications for This Class
For a tiller-steered sailboat of this size (31 ft, ~10,700 lbs displacement), suitable autopilot systems typically include:

- **Raymarine ST1000+**: Entry-level, rated to 6,600 lbs (under-spec for Miura)
- **Raymarine EV-100 Tiller**: Rated to 13,200 lbs displacement (appropriate)
- **Simrad TP10**: Designed for boats up to 33 ft / 3,700 kg (marginal for Miura)
- **Pelagic PA1**: Professional-grade system with 9-axis gyro for tiller vessels

### Design Considerations for Autotiller Systems
The Miura 31's characteristics relevant to automatic tiller systems:
- Moderate displacement requires adequate autopilot power
- Transom-hung rudder configuration
- Tiller steering mechanism
- Good offshore capability suggests autopilot usage scenarios
- Balanced sail plan with genoa-dominant power
